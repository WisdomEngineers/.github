Admin eats hours. Staff use ChatGPT with no shared rules. The app you built breaks. Nobody senior looks after the software the business runs on.

We're two founders in Ireland who fix that. Wisdom Engineers is a technical partner for Irish SMEs that know AI and software matter but have no senior technical lead. We teach your people, build what is worth building, rescue what was built badly, and keep it running.

## What we have built

Our own products and tools, not client work. Each case study sets out the problem, what we built, what it measured, and what we handed over or retired.

- **[Slipway](https://wisdomengineers.ie/work/slipway/)**: live 13 days after the prototype review. It is a crewing app for retained firefighters, built for the fire station where Piotr serves as a retained firefighter. As of 10 October 2026, the crew use it at no charge (self-reported). All 11 serving members of the crew had claimed an account by 7 October 2026. It is not the official record of availability. Its own site is [slipway.ie](https://slipway.ie/).
- **[Tallywise](https://wisdomengineers.ie/work/tallywise/)**: an AI model reads the paperwork, plain code does the sums. It keeps our own books. A person checks every AI reading. As of 15 October 2026, for seven filed VAT3 periods, the figures it computes agree with what was filed on every box. It is an internal tool: not tax advice, and not a product we sell.
- **[Handee](https://wisdomengineers.ie/work/handee/)**: first tagged release 24 days after the first commit. It was our own software product, built by one founder. It had no paying customers between its production release on 4 July 2026 and 30 August 2026 (self-reported). That day we switched it off on running cost. We built before we sold, and that was our mistake. Now we test demand first.

Our code stays private, so there is little to browse here. We show the work in our [Insights](https://wisdomengineers.ie/insights/) instead: what we built, what went wrong and what we changed.

## How we work

Technology doesn't save time on its own: automate a messy process, and you get the same mess, faster.

- **We start with the problem.** We look at how your people, processes and systems fit together. Often the fix is to remove a step.
- **We test before we promise.** Every engagement includes a small trial of the fix, run on your real work rather than on a sample.
- **We tackle boring problems.** Admin is nobody's favourite thing to fix, but it eats hours. So we give it the attention it needs.
- **A founder does the work.** You deal with the two of us, not a team you haven't met.
- **Scope and price are fixed.** If the work takes us longer than planned, that's our cost, not yours. When a date has to move, we tell you early, and the price stays the same.
- **A written handover comes with every engagement**, so you can carry on with us or without us.

Even this GitHub organisation's settings are kept as code. The code is tested, and a scheduled check compares the live settings with it twice a day.
